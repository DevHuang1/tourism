Travel FAQs

Everything you need to know before, during, and after your trip

Do I need a visa to visit these countries? ❯
Visa rules depend on your nationality. Check the embassy website or contact us for updated guidance to avoid last-minute surprises.
What lodging options are available? ❯
We offer hotels suitable for every budget and preference.
How can I get around in a new city? ❯
Choose from public transport, taxis, rideshare apps, or bike rentals. We provide local guides and tips for easy navigation!
Should I carry cash or cards? ❯
A mix works best. Cards are widely accepted in cities, but carry cash for markets, street vendors, and remote areas.
How should I pack for different climates? ❯
Layering is key. Lightweight clothes for warm areas, a jacket for cooler regions, and compact rain gear. Check local forecasts before you pack!
How can I experience authentic local food? ❯
Visit local markets, family-run restaurants, and join food tours for a true taste of the destination.
What are the top Instagram-worthy spots? ❯
- Myanmar: Bagan sunrise with hot air balloons
- Japan: Kyoto Fushimi Inari gates
- Switzerland: Lauterbrunnen waterfalls
What local festivals should I experience? ❯
- Myanmar: Thingyan Water Festival
- Vietnam: Tet Lunar New Year
- Japan: Gion Matsuri
- Switzerland: Fasnacht Carnival
Unique souvenirs to buy? ❯
- Myanmar: Lacquerware & puppets
- Thailand: Silk & handmade crafts
- Japan: Matcha & quirky gadgets
- Switzerland: Swiss chocolate & watches
How do I respect local culture? ❯
Dress modestly for religious sites, learn simple local phrases, and respect customs.
Time zone tips for scheduling activities? ❯
Adjust to local time immediately upon arrival, schedule early sightseeing, and avoid planning too much on the first day.
How to stay safe during natural disasters? ❯
Research local emergency procedures for floods, typhoons, or snowstorms. Follow alerts from local authorities.
