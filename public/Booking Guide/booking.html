Booking Guide

Ready to explore? Here's your complete guide to booking tours on our website, from selecting your destination to checking your confirmation

✅ Step 1: Browse and Choose a Tour

  • Go to the Tours section and explore the available destinations.
  • Click on book now to see the available flights .
  • Or you can go to the Book section to directly select your desire destination and check details like itinerary, inclusions, and price

✅ Step 2: Select Your Travel Date & Guests

  • Pick your preferred date from the calendar.
  • Enter the number of travelers (adults/children).

✅ Step 3: Provide Traveler Details

  • Fill in guest information such as name and contact details.
  • No account is required — booking is done as a guest.

✅ Step 4: Make Payment

  • Enter your credit card details (8-digit card supported on the site).
  • Payment is processed securely.

✅ Step 5: Receive Confirmation

  • Once your payment is successful, you’ll receive a confirmation message saying "Booking Confirmed".
  • You can view your reservation anytime in the "My Bookings" section.

✅ Step 6: Manage Your Booking

  • Use the “My Bookings” option to view details, confirm, or see cancellation policies.
