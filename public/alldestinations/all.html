Premier Destinations In Cambodia

Angkor Wat

A UNESCO World Heritage site and the largest religious monument in the world. Ideal for sunrise tours and historical exploration. Famous for sunrise tours and intricate carvings, Angkor Wat is the heart of Cambodia’s history and culture.

Siem Reap 1-1.5 hours

Bayon Temple

Bayon Temple is famous for its massive stone faces smiling across the temple grounds. It is filled with detailed bas-reliefs that depict ancient Khmer life and mythology. Visitors can explore its corridors, towers, and hidden courtyards full of mystery.

Angkor Thom Half Day

Phonom Penh

Phnom Penh is a lively capital where tradition meets modern life. Key highlights include the Royal Palace, Silver Pagoda, and bustling markets. The riverside offers cafes, shops, and stunning sunset views.

Phnom Penh 1-2 Days

Koh Rong Island

Koh Rong Island is a tropical escape with soft white beaches and turquoise waters. It is perfect for snorkeling, diving, or relaxing under swaying palm trees. At night, glowing plankton lights up the sea for a magical experience.

Sihanoukville 2-3 hours

Tonlé Sap Lake

Tonlé Sap is the largest freshwater lake in Southeast Asia and a UNESCO biosphere reserve. It is famous for its floating villages, stilt houses, and flooded forests that change with the seasons. A boat ride offers a glimpse into local life, fishing traditions, and the unique ecosystem that sustains millions.

Siem Reap Province 2-3 Days

Prominent Destinations In China

Great Wall of China

Hike ancient watchtowers,ride a cable car,or zoom down on a toboggan-the Great Wall offers epic views and a true step into China's past.Each stone tells a story of strength,legacy and breathtaking endurance.

Huairou or Yangqing district,Beijing 4-5 hours

Shanghai

In Shanghai,tradition and modernity collide.Wander the bund for skyline,head up the Shanghai Tower,and explore ancient beauty at Yuyuan Garden.Take a river cruise by night,then shop and snack your way through Nanjiang Road.

Pudong and Huangpu Districts 1-2 Days

Forbidden City

Once home to emperors, the Forbidden City is full of grand halls,secret gardens, and golden rooftops. Walk through majesic gates,explore the opulent palaces,and discover the treasures of China's imperial past which are embedded in the heart of Beijing.

Dongcheng District 3-4 hours

Chongqing

Known as the "Mountain City," Chongqing is famous for its spicy hotpot,stunning night views,and the Yangtze River cruise. Explore the ancient streets of Ciqikou,visit the Three Gorges Museum,and take a scenic boat ride through the dramatic gorges.

Chongqing Municipality 2-3 Days

Lijiang

Lijiang is a UNESCO World Heritage site known for its well-preserved ancient town,traditional Naxi culture,and stunning natural scenery. Explore the cobblestone streets,visit the Black Dragon Pool,and hike in the nearby Jade Dragon Snow Mountain.

Lijiang City,Yunnan Province 2-3 Days

Featured Destinations In Norway

Northern Lights in Tromsø

Tromsø is ideal for witnessing the magical Northern Lights.Visitors can take guided aurora-chasing tours by bus,snowmobile,or dog-sled,offering unforgettable views of the dancing green lights in the polar sky.

Tromsø 2-3 Days

Hjørundfjorden

It is nestled among the Sunnmø Alps,is perfect for peaceful fjord cruises,kayaking,and scenic hikes.Surrounded by dramatic mountains and tranquil water,it's a hidden gem for nature lovers and photographers.

Møre og Romsdal 1-2 Days

Lofoten Islands

Wonderful Lofoten Islands offer breathtaking scenery with rugged peaks,fishing villages, and white sand beaches.Visitors can enjoy activities like hiking,cycling,kayaking and even surfing under the midnight sun or northern lights,depending on the season.

Nordland 3-5 days

Loen Skylift

It whisks you from the fjord to the mountaintop in just minutes,revealing sweeping views of Nordfjord below.At the top,you can hike,dine or simply relax while taking in some of Norway's most awe-insping landscapes.

Vestland,Western Norway 2-4 hours

Trolltunga

Trolltunga is one of Norway's most iconic hikes,leading to a dramatic rock ledge that juts out 700 meters above Ringedalsvatnet Lake.Adventurers can embark on the full-day hike for a rewarding,panoramic photo opportunity at the edge of the cliff.

Vestland(near Odda) 1-2 Days

Featured Destinations In Singapore

Marina Bay Sands

Marina Bay Sands is an architectural icon with its three towers and rooftop SkyPark. It features luxury hotels, an infinity pool, a casino, and high-end shopping. At night, the area lights up with spectacular skyline views.

Marina Bay, Central Singapore 1 Days

Gardens by the Bay

Gardens by the Bay is a futuristic nature park famous for its Supertree Grove and Flower Dome. The evening light shows turn the gardens into a dazzling spectacle. Visitors can stroll along the Skyway to enjoy breathtaking city views

Marina Bay, Central Singapore. 1 Days

Merlion Park

The Merlion, a half-lion, half-fish statue, is Singapore’s most iconic symbol. Located by Marina Bay, it represents the city’s fishing village roots and lion legend. It is a must-visit photo spot for travelers.

One Fullerton, near Marina Bay. 1 days

S.E.A. Aquarium

The S.E.A. Aquarium is one of the world’s largest aquariums, home to over 100,000 marine animals. Visitors can explore diverse habitats, from coral reefs to open-ocean tanks. It offers both educational and breathtaking underwater experiences.

Resorts World Sentosa 1-2 Days

Universal Studios Singapore

Universal Studios Singapore brings Hollywood magic to life with thrilling rides and shows. Each zone offers a unique theme, from Jurassic Park to Transformers. It’s a full-day adventure for families, friends, and movie lovers.

Resorts World Sentosa 1-2 Days

Featured Destinations In Switzerland

Interlaken

Nestled between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, Interlaken is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts. Known for its adventure sports, visitors can enjoy hiking, paragliding, and skiing in the surrounding mountains.

Interlaken-Oberhasli,Canton of Bern 2-3 Days

Lauterbrunnen

Lauterbrunnen is a stunning valley known for its 72 waterfalls, including the famous Staubbach Falls. The picturesque village serves as a gateway to the Jungfrau region, offering breathtaking views and hiking trails.

Interlaken-Oberhasli,Canton of Bern 1-2 Days

Lake Blausee

Lake Blausee is a picturesque alpine lake known for its crystal-clear turquoise waters and surrounding forest. Visitors can enjoy hiking trails, picnicking by the lake, or simply relaxing in this serene natural setting.

Frutigen-Niedersimmental,Canton of Bern 2-4 hours

Bern

The capital city of Switzerland, Bern is known for its well-preserved medieval architecture and charming old town, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visitors can explore the Zytglogge clock tower, the Federal Palace, and the Bear Park, or stroll along the Aare River.

Bern-Mittelland,Canton of Bern 1-2 days

St.Moritz

St. Moritz is a world-renowned luxury resort town in the Swiss Alps, famous for its winter sports and upscale amenities. Visitors can enjoy skiing, snowboarding, and ice skating in the winter, or hiking, mountain biking, and sailing in the summer.

Maloja,Canton of Graubünden 1-2 Days

Ho Chi Minh City

A dynamic metropolis where modern skyscrapers meet French colonial architecture, Ho Chi Minh City offers bustling markets, vibrant nightlife, and historic landmarks like the War Remnants Museum and Notre Dame Cathedral.

Ha Long Bay

A UNESCO World Heritage site, Ha Long Bay is famous for its emerald waters and thousands of towering limestone islands topped with rainforests. Visitors can enjoy cruises, kayaking, cave exploration, and breathtaking sunsets.
